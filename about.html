Our Story

North Star Bakery started as a small family kitchen and grew into a neighborhood gathering place. We still bake every item by hand, the same way we did when we opened our doors.

Sourcing Philosophy

We source flour, eggs, and dairy from farms within a hundred miles whenever possible, and we bake in small batches to keep everything fresh.

Meet the Team

Our head baker has over fifteen years of experience, and our pastry team trained locally before joining North Star Bakery.
